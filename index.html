About Us

About LEGENGLOBAL

Who We Are

LEGENGLOBAL INC. is a California-based international travel and cultural exchange company, a subsidiary of Sihai Travels. We specialize in bridging Eastern and Western cultures through comprehensive travel services and cultural programs.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional international travel experiences while fostering cultural understanding between nations. We aim to be the premier bridge connecting travelers with authentic global experiences.

Our Experience

With decades of combined experience in the travel industry, our team has successfully organized countless tours, cultural exchanges, and business conferences across multiple continents.

Our Network

We maintain strong partnerships with airlines, hotels, cultural institutions, and government agencies worldwide, ensuring seamless service delivery for all your travel needs.
